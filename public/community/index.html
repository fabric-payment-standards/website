About This Space

A neutral forum for open collaboration

The Fabric Payment Standards Community Hub is the primary venue for discussion, coordination, and collective decision-making across all active working groups and specification tracks.

Participation is open to any individual or organisation committed to the Foundation's principles of vendor neutrality, interoperability, and transparent governance.
