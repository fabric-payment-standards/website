Project Update

StableCoin Stack has a new home.

This project has joined a broader initiative. The StableCoin Stack Foundation spec is now hosted under Fabric Payment Standards — a unified umbrella advancing open standards for the future of digital payments.

stablecoinstack.org → fabricpaymentstandards.org/specs/ssf

What changed

bigger vision.

The StableCoin Stack Foundation (SSF) specification you know hasn't changed — it's simply been brought into the Fabric Payment Standards umbrella to align with a wider ecosystem of open payment protocols.

All previous links, documentation, and references to stablecoinstack.org should be updated to point to the new URL. This page will remain active as a permanent redirect.
