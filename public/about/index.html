About FabricPayment Standards

An independent, non-profit body dedicated to open, interoperable payment infrastructure — governed by its participants, beholden to no single vendor.

Mission

Open standards forreal-world payments

The global payments landscape is increasingly fragmented. Proprietary protocols, closed networks, and competing vendor ecosystems impose unnecessary friction on the organisations and individuals who depend on reliable value transfer.

Fabric Payment Standards exists to produce and maintain rigorous, vendor-neutral specifications that any implementer — from a central bank to a startup — can adopt without licence fees, lock-in, or permission from a commercial gatekeeper.

The Foundation operates as a neutral convener: its specifications are developed in the open, its governance is multi-stakeholder, and its outputs are published under permissive terms that maximise real-world adoption.

Principles
⚖

Neutral Governance

No participant class — vendor, financial institution, or government — holds a controlling interest. Decisions require broad consensus.

🔓

Open Process

All specification work is conducted in public. Meeting minutes, votes, and decision rationales are published as a matter of policy.

🌍

Real-World Focus

Specifications are evaluated against deployment reality, not theoretical elegance. Implementer feedback drives revision cycles.

🔗

Interoperability First

Every design decision is measured against its impact on cross-implementation compatibility and long-term ecosystem coherence.
